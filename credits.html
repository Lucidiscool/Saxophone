The sound

FluidR3 GM saxophone samples, rendered by MIDI.js Soundfonts / gleitz, provided under CC BY 3.0 as documented by that project. Samples are played at adjusted pitches. Rare family members use pitch-shifted soprano or baritone samples; these are register demonstrations rather than authentic recordings of each rare saxophone.

Hold a key to play; release it to stop. Sample recordings have a finite length. Written notes span C4–C6 and sound at the selected instrument’s transposition. Rare experimental instruments vary in design and tuning.

Further reading

Yamaha’s guide to the saxophone family introduces the most established family members. The broader collection also includes the rare sopranissimo, contrabass, and subcontrabass registers.
